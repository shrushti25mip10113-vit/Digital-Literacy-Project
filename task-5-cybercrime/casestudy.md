UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes in India today. 

In this type of fraud, scammers trick users into transferring money or sharing sensitive information such as OTPs or PINs.

A typical scenario involves a fraudster calling or messaging a victim while pretending to be a bank official, customer support agent, or even a buyer/seller from an online platform. 

The scammer may send a fake payment request or ask the victim to scan a QR code, claiming that money will be received. 

In reality, when the victim approves the request or enters their PIN, money is deducted from their account.

Students and elderly individuals are often targeted because they may not be fully aware of such scams.

The consequences of UPI fraud include financial loss, stress, and loss of trust in digital platforms. 

In many cases, victims are unable to recover their money if the fraud is not reported immediately.

This case study highlights the importance of awareness and careful handling of digital payment systems. 

Users must always verify requests and avoid sharing confidential information online.
